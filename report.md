# Final Business Report: Pharmaceutical Market & Prescription Analytics

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes the NHS Prescription Cost Analysis (PCA) monthly dataset to identify trends in pharmaceutical dispensing and expenditure across England. The primary goal is to surface commercial opportunities and identify segments that warrant further investigation by pharmaceutical and healthcare stakeholders.

## 2. Business Objectives
- **Market & Cost**: Identify therapeutic categories with the highest prescription volumes and expenditures.
- **Geography**: Evaluate prescription activity variances across Integrated Care Boards (ICBs).
- **Trend & Forecast**: Analyze historical growth patterns to forecast future demand.
- **Commercial Insights**: Provide actionable recommendations on potential areas for commercial investment and detailed product performance (Cost per Item & Average Cost per Quantity).

## 3. Methodology
- **Data Source**: NHSBSA Open Data Portal (Open Government Licence 3.0).
- **Timeframe**: July 2026 (Initial focus) / 2024–2026 (Full analysis).
- **Tools**: Python (EDA & ML), SQL (Aggregation), Power BI (Visualization).
